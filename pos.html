Help & Support

Find answers to common issues or contact our support team.

How do I add items to the cart?

Navigate to the 'Menu' tab, select your category, and click the '+ ADD' button on any product.

Can I change the quantity of an item?

Yes! In the 'Current Order' panel, use the + and - buttons or directly edit the number box.

How do I update tax or currency settings?

Go to the 'Setting' tab from the sidebar, update your desired values, and click 'Save Settings'.
